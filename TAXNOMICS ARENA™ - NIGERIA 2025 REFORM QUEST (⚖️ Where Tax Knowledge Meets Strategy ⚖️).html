📖 HOW TO PLAY — TAXNOMICS ARENA™

🎯 Objective

Navigate Nigeria's tax landscape, answer questions from the 4 Nigeria Tax Acts 2025, earn Compliance Points, and become the ultimate Tax Champion. The player with the highest Compliance Score wins!

🎮 Game Flow

  • Set up 1–5 players, enter names and pick avatars.
  • Each turn: Roll the dice to determine your Tax Zone category.
  • Answer a question from that zone's Act/topic to earn points.
  • Correct answer = +Compliance Points & positive pop-up insight.
  • Wrong answer = -Points & non-compliance warning pop-up.
  • Special zones (🎁 Bonus, ⚠️ Penalty) trigger automatic events.
  • Game ends after all players complete their rounds (default: 8 rounds each).

📊 Scoring

  • Easy Question: +10 pts correct / -5 pts wrong
  • Medium Question: +20 pts correct / -10 pts wrong
  • Hard Question: +35 pts correct / -15 pts wrong
  • Bonus Zone: +25 pts automatic
  • Penalty Zone: -20 pts automatic

🏆 Tax Zones

  • 💚 Income Tax — Companies & Individual taxation
  • 🔵 VAT — Value Added Tax provisions
  • 🟡 Capital Gains — Assets, gains & disposal
  • 🟣 Tax Admin — Registration, filing & compliance
  • 🟠 Petroleum — Hydrocarbon & oil sector tax
  • ⚪ Stamp Duty — Dutiable instruments
  • 🔴 Penalty — Automatic point deduction!
  • ⭐ Bonus — Automatic point reward!

🏅 Milestones

  • 🌱 Tax Seedling — First correct answer
  • 📋 Compliance Star — 3 consecutive correct answers
  • 🏛️ Act Scholar — Answer from all 4 Acts
  • 💰 Tax Champion — Reach 100+ points
  • ⚖️ Grand Arbiter — Finish with 90%+ compliance rate
